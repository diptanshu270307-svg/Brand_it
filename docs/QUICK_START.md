# BrandLint Quick Start Guide (Nontechnical)

Welcome to **BrandLint**! BrandLint helps marketing, communications, and product teams ensure their public copy is accurate, on-brand, and backed by verifiable proof before publishing.

---

## 1. What BrandLint Does

When writing promotional blog posts, social announcements, emails, or investor copy, it's easy for outdated figures, unverified claims, or off-tone phrases to slip in. 

BrandLint acts as your team's real-time review assistant:
- **Checks your copy in seconds**: Paste your draft, pick your channel (e.g., Blog, LinkedIn, Instagram, Email), and click **Check content**.
- **Plain-language feedback**: Issues are clearly categorized into:
  - **Must fix** (Critical blockers: unverified claims, revoked proof, legal disclaimers).
  - **Needs review** (Tone inconsistencies, unconfirmed numbers, channel guidelines).
  - **Suggestions** (Helpful word-choice improvements you can accept in one click).
- **Evidence Passports**: An Evidence Passport is simply *the approved source behind a claim* (such as a benchmark study, customer case study, or audited report).
- **Human approval workflow**: When your draft is clean, submit it for review. An independent teammate or compliance lead reviews and approves it before publication.

## 2. Getting Started in 5 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Sign In or Register
1. Open BrandLint in your web browser.
2. If invited by a teammate, click the invitation link in your email to choose a secure password and join your team workspace.
3. If trying a local demo, click **Switch persona** to experience the app as **Casey** (Content Creator), **Riley** (Reviewer), or **Olivia** (Brand Owner).

### Step 2: Set Up Your Brand Guidelines (Owners Only)
1. Go to **Brand rules** in the top navigation.
2. Click **Upload guideline** and upload your team's style guide PDF or paste text rules.
3. Inspect the extracted rules (e.g. prohibited terminology, tone constraints) and click **Approve** to activate them.

### Step 3: Register an Approved Fact
Promotional copy often mentions statistics or metrics (e.g., *"Reduces latency by 30%"*).
1. Go to **Approved facts** in the navigation.
2. Click **Add approved fact**.
3. Enter the exact claim statement and cite the source document and page number.
4. Once verified, this fact becomes an active **Evidence Passport** that validates your drafts automatically.

### Step 4: Check Your First Draft
1. Click **Check content** in the navigation bar.
2. Enter a draft title (e.g., *"Spring Product Announcement"*), choose your channel (e.g., *Blog*), and paste your text into the editor.
3. Click **Check content**.
4. BrandLint highlights issues directly in your text:
   - Click any highlighted card to view the plain-language explanation and rule version.
   - For suggestions, click **Accept edit** to apply the fix safely while preserving protected product names, numbers, and CTAs.

### Step 5: Request Team Approval
1. Once all **Must fix** blockers are resolved, click **Request approval**.
2. BrandLint automatically routes your draft to an eligible independent reviewer on your team.
3. Your reviewer receives an in-app notification to review and sign off.

## 3. Key Concepts in Plain English

| Term | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Evidence Passport** | The approved source behind a specific claim (document, page, excerpt, and validity dates). |
| **Guideline Health** | Feedback on rules your team frequently disagrees with or overrides, helping owners refine unclear guidelines. |
| **Channel Condition** | A rule exemption for specific media (e.g., casual greetings allowed on Instagram, but restricted in Investor copy). |
| **Evidence Recall** | An automatic safety recall: if supporting evidence expires or is revoked, any previously approved drafts citing it are marked *Needs revalidation* so your team never publishes invalid claims. |
| **Protected Entities** | Your official product names, legal disclaimers, and calls-to-action that rewrites are strictly prevented from modifying. |

## 4. Need Help?

- **Insights**: Check **Insights** to view how your team guidelines are performing and which rules are working smoothly.
- **Support**: Reach out to your team workspace administrator (Brand Owner) for permission changes or workspace questions.
